How Zero-Trust Device Control Protects Cardiac Records

In a critical heart care environment, patient ECG waveforms, catheterization logs, and anti-arrhythmic prescriptions must not be accessed from unvetted consumer devices or rogue laptops.

1. Hardened Clinical Terminals

Registered ICU terminals (e.g., DEV-DOC01-TRUSTED) are bound via MAC/device tokens, reducing the baseline risk score by -5 points.

2. Unknown External Laptops

Any session initiated from an unverified terminal (e.g., DEV-UNKNOWN-EXT-88) incurs an immediate +20 penalty, driving the user closer to honeypot diversion.

3. Dynamic Session Re-evaluation

Toggling a device instantly re-calibrates future requests from that terminal without requiring database restarts or cache flushes.
